# Retention and legal-hold authority

Labels: `wayfinder:grilling`
Status: open
Map: `../map.md`

## Question

What local retention, deletion, and legal-hold policy governs immutable GSC/GA4
evidence bundles before the agency delivery slice can automate recurring
reports?

The answer must name the default retention window, who may authorize deletion,
how a hold is represented, and what the system does when a bundle is missing or
expired. It must not assume hosted storage or legal requirements that the
operator has not confirmed.

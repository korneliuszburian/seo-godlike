# Multi-tenant onboarding authority

Labels: `wayfinder:research`
Status: closed
Map: `../map.md`

## Question

What operator evidence and approval workflow is required to bind a client to a
canonical property and aliases, while preserving the one-agency-account model
and preventing accidental cross-tenant access?

## Resolution

Onboarding authority remains local and proof-driven: each provider/property
gets an explicit capability record and proof state. GA4 is the next exercise of
that state machine; no ownership is inferred from discovery or documentation.

Research disposition: `tenant-onboarding-authority-capability-state-machine`.
